The main goal of my non-working major qualifying project was to have a competitve autonomous robot bully highschoolers playing the VEX V5 Robotics Competition game. The 2025-26 game was called "Push Back" and we totally blew it since it was actually super achievable to have a minimum viable product. The [game]( https://content.vexrobotics.com/docs/25-26/v5rc-push-back/docs/Push-Back-4.0.pdf ) itself was one of the better ones for autonomy since it did not require a complicated mechanical design.
